Why research?

I have received warmth from many people along the way. Research is one way I hope to pass that warmth forward — not as a slogan, but as a practice.

My current interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, neuroscience, and healthcare. I want to build systems that help us understand neural signals and, one day, improve the lives of patients and families.

Advisor: Prof. Ran Wang, Biomedical Engineering, Nanjing University.
