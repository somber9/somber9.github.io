Current Research

BrainNeRF: EEG Spatial Super-Resolution

BrainNeRF aims to reconstruct high-density EEG signals from sparse electrode observations. The project explores continuous spatial representations, temporal basis learning, and physics-guided modeling for neural signal reconstruction.

  • EEG spatial super-resolution
  • Continuous neural field representation
  • Physics-guided learning
  • Frequency-aware evaluation
