Current Research

BrainNeRF

Physics-guided Neural Field for EEG Spatial Super-Resolution. The project studies how to reconstruct high-density EEG signals from sparse electrode observations using continuous neural representations and temporal basis learning.

Observed16-channel EEG
PriorPhysics guidance
FieldContinuous neural representation
TemporalDCT basis
Target32-channel EEG

Motivation

High-density EEG can provide richer spatial information, but dense electrode systems are often less convenient in real-world BCI settings. EEG spatial super-resolution aims to recover missing channels from sparse observations.

Method

  • Continuous spatial neural field representation
  • Physics-guided learning for neural signal reconstruction
  • DCT temporal basis representation
  • Frequency-aware evaluation with PSD and band-power metrics

Evaluation

  • Time-domain metrics: RMSE, MAE, Pearson correlation, R²
  • Frequency-domain metrics: PSD MAE, PSD correlation, band power
  • Subject-aware evaluation to reduce data leakage risk
  • Downstream analysis for emotion and neural signal tasks

Status

Active Manuscript and experiments are in progress. Target venue: AAAI.
